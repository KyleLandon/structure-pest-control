05 FAQ

Common questions.

Still have one? Call us at (830) 555-0123 and a real person will pick up.

Are your treatments safe for kids and pets?

Yes. We select products and application methods with families, pets and livestock in mind, and we'll tell you exactly what we're using and any precautions to take before we begin.

How often should I have my home treated?

Recurring plans are monthly or quarterly. Home Protection covers the interior and exterior. Home + Lawn Protection adds annual fire ant protection, and Total Property Protection adds seasonal mosquito protection. We'll recommend a plan after a free inspection.

Do you offer one-time treatments or do I need a contract?

Both. Specialty treatments are available for a specific problem. Recurring plans start with a 12-month term and renew each year. You can cancel with 30 days' notice, and there is no cancellation fee.

What does rodent control involve?

Rodent control is a specialty service. We inspect how rodents are getting in, remove the current population, and can seal the openings you authorize. Exclusion covers entry doors, garage doors, wall penetrations, and plumbing voids.

Which pests are covered by Home Protection?

Common household ants, cockroaches other than German cockroaches, spiders, scorpions, crickets, silverfish, earwigs, and similar occasional invaders. German cockroaches, bed bugs, fleas, ticks, rodents, and mosquitoes are specialty services unless your plan includes them.

What happens if pests come back after a treatment?

On an active recurring plan, if a covered pest comes back, so do we. Tell us and we'll inspect and treat it between regular visits while your account is current. One-time and specialty services are handled separately.
