05 FAQ

Common questions.

Still have one? Call us at (830) 555-0123 and a real person will pick up.

Are your treatments safe for kids and pets?

We select products and treatment methods with your family, pets, and property in mind. Your technician will explain any precautions you need to take before treatment begins.

How often should I have my home treated?

Every property is different. We offer different service intervals based on your property, pest activity, and the level of protection you need. We'll recommend the right approach after evaluating your property.

Do you offer one-time and recurring pest control?

Yes. For most homes, we recommend ongoing pest control to provide consistent protection throughout the year. Our residential recurring service does not require a long-term contract. We also offer one-time treatments when that is a better fit for the pest problem or your needs.

What does rodent control involve?

Rodent problems can require more than simply removing the rodents you see. We inspect for activity and potential entry points, address the current problem, and can recommend exclusion work to help prevent future issues.

Which pests are covered by Home Protection?

Home Protection covers many of the common pests found around South Texas homes, including ants, cockroaches, spiders, scorpions, crickets, silverfish, earwigs, and other occasional invaders. Certain pests, including German cockroaches, bed bugs, fleas, ticks, rodents, and mosquitoes, require specialized treatment or separate coverage.

What happens if pests come back after a treatment?

If a covered pest comes back between your regularly scheduled services, so do we, at no additional cost. Just give us a call and we'll take care of it. One-time and specialty services may have different warranty terms.
