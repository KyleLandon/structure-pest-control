06 FAQ

Common questions.

Still have one? Call us at (830) 555-0123 and a real person will pick up.

Are your treatments safe for kids and pets?

Yes. We select products and application methods with families, pets and livestock in mind, and we'll tell you exactly what we're using and any precautions to take before we begin.

How often should I have my home treated?

For most South Texas homes, Essential ($44.99/mo after a $149 first visit) keeps common pests under control with quarterly exterior service. Yards with fire ants, fleas, or ticks usually do better on Complete ($69.99/mo). Acreage, shops, and barns are quoted on Total Property, from $99.99/mo. We'll recommend a plan after a free inspection.

Do you offer one-time treatments or do I need a contract?

Both. One-time treatments are available for specific problems, and recurring plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract required.

What does rodent control involve?

We start with an inspection to find how rodents are getting in and where they're active. We then remove the current population with trapping or baiting, seal entry points, and set up monitoring to catch any new activity early.

Do you service businesses and commercial properties?

Yes. We provide scheduled, documented service for restaurants, offices, retail stores, warehouses and agricultural operations, with flexible timing to avoid disrupting your business.

What happens if pests come back after a treatment?

Our work is guaranteed. If pests return between scheduled visits on a maintenance plan, we'll come back and re-treat at no additional charge.
